**Also produce the scientifically comparable engineering stress–strain curve**: per saved converged increment take signed top/bottom axial resultant reaction F(N) and platen or top displacement u(mm), compute positive compression strain = |u|/H0 and positive engineering stress = |F|/A_full_outer (MPa).
