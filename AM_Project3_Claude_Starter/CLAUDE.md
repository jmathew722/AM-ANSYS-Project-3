Required pipeline: install/interface preflight; CAD audit; material XML audit and assignment to all solid bodies; physical area and density calculations; physically justified load selection; force-ramped test (1000 N is an initial *candidate*, not fixed); stronger displacement-driven yield test if required; mesh convergence; non-linear solve and reaction/history exports; two distinct stress–strain outputs (tutorial's **max equivalent stress versus max equivalent total strain** and engineering **F/A_gross versus u/H**); yield point red circle with numerical annotation; Excel formulas; rank yield/relative-density; output verification; two-page preliminary FEM summary.

Crucial: a pair of maximum local equivalent stress/strain values are generally *not* a specimen's engineering stress–strain curve, and maxima may come from different locations. Do not infer material yielding just because local stress exceeds the constituent yield. Explain distinction and use engineering yield as the ranking metric. Do not equate the tutorial screenshot's 213 MPa (steel example) to Tough 2000 yield.

Block or label as *NOT VALIDATED* if: missing geometry, missing material density, failed contact/mesh, no credible yield criterion, no load steps, failed mesh convergence, missing literature validation, or corrupted exports. Never fabricate results or screenshots. Record assumptions, uncertainties, solver logs, versions, and per-design validation status.
